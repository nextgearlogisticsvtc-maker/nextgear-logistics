# Paintbook Rules

Welcome to the NextGear Logistics Paintbook.

This Paintbook contains all approved truck paintjobs that may be used by NextGear Logistics drivers. The collection includes multiple paint designs for different truck brands and models, allowing drivers to personalize their vehicle while maintaining a professional and recognizable company appearance.

These paintjobs are approved for:
* Daily driving
* Deliveries
* Official company convoys
* Community events
* Other approved occasions

---

### Official Company Colors
* All paintjobs in this Paintbook use the official NextGear Logistics color scheme.
* The official colors, striping, and company identity may not be altered or replaced unless leadership has given explicit approval.

### Alternative Colors
* Drivers may only use alternative colors on an official NextGear Logistics paintjob after receiving approval from Leadership (this only applies to Company Activity).
* Without approval, only the standard Paintbook versions may be used.

### Sharing & Distribution
* Sharing, distributing, leaking, or reproducing Paintbook designs outside of NextGear Logistics is strictly prohibited.
* These paintjobs are exclusive to NextGear Logistics.

### Abuse
* Any abuse or intentional misuse of the Paintbook or its designs is forbidden.
* Violations, including unauthorized sharing or modification of official paintjobs, may result in disciplinary action, including a strike, suspension of Paintbook access, or further action depending on the severity.

{% hint style="info" %}
By following these guidelines, every driver helps maintain the professional image and identity of NextGear Logistics across the roads of Euro Truck Simulator 2.
{% endhint %}
