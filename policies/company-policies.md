# Company Policies

To maintain a professional and safe environment, NextGear Logistics has clear policies that all drivers must follow. Upholding these ensures smooth operations and fair treatment for everyone.

### Disciplinary Actions
Disciplinary measures exist to keep our community safe, professional, and enjoyable. Each case is reviewed individually, considering the situation and circumstances. Actions may include:

* **Warning** — For minor offenses or first-time violations.
* **Event Suspension** — For repeated or serious rule breaches.
* **Removal** — For repeated or major violations that compromise safety or professionalism.

### Professional Conduct
Always behave respectfully in-game and on Discord. Avoid arguments, trolling, or negative interactions with other drivers or VTCs.

Use your driver tag correctly: `NextGear [XX]` (where XX is your country code). This identifies you properly in convoys and public servers.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Why it matters:** Following company policies ensures fairness, professionalism, and trust within NextGear. Every driver's behavior contributes to our reputation and community strength.
{% endhint %}
