# Company History

Founded by passionate drivers, NextGear Logistics was built with one mission: to create a company where every driver is respected, supported, and part of a family.

Through the times, we've grown into a recognized and trusted name on TruckersMP, known for professional convoys, safe driving, and engaging community events.

Joining NextGear means becoming part of a long-standing tradition of excellence, where your professionalism, teamwork, and dedication help shape the company's reputation.

**Why it matters:** Knowing our history helps you understand our values, culture, standards and encourages pride in being part of a driver-focused community.
