# Company Standards

Your truck and trailer are rolling billboards for NextGear Logistics — they represent our professionalism, reputation, and attention to detail. Maintaining a clean, properly branded vehicle is mandatory for all drivers.

* **Approved Paint Jobs Only** — Only use company-approved skins or presets. This prevents visual confusion and keeps all vehicles looking uniform and professional.
* **Special Events & Themes** — Occasionally, events may require themed vehicles. Participating shows your flexibility and commitment to community engagement.
* **Professional Conduct** — Always be polite and respectful both in-game and on Discord. Your actions reflect not only on you but on NextGear and our partner VTCs.
* **Driver Tag Rule** — Once you're a driver for NextGear Logistics, you must always wear the correct tag: `NextGear [XX]`, where XX is your country code. This ensures proper identification in convoys, public servers, and during events.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Why it matters:** A professional appearance, conduct, and proper driver tag strengthen our company's reputation, improve convoy coordination, and make every event safer and more enjoyable for all drivers.
{% endhint %}
