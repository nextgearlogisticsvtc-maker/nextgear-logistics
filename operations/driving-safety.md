# Driving & Safety

At NextGear Logistics, safe driving is a top priority. Following these guidelines ensures smooth convoys, professional operations, and the safety of all drivers. Remember: safety isn't just about avoiding accidents — it's about protecting yourself and your fellow drivers.

* **Route Planning** — Always plan your route in advance. Check for fuel stops, rest areas, and avoid unsafe zones during peak hours unless convoyed. A well-prepared driver is a reliable teammate.
* **Speed & Compliance** — Always follow TMP speed limits and adjust for weather or road conditions. Speeding increases risk and can disrupt convoys.
* **Accident Procedure** — If you encounter a reckless driver or crash:
  * Stop in a safe location.
  * Inform the Convoy Leader.
  * Record evidence (screenshots/clips).
  * Report to TMP and NextGear staff.
* **Vehicle Maintenance & Awareness** — Keep your truck roadworthy, clean, and fully fueled. Check mirrors frequently, anticipate traffic, and stay alert at all times.
* **Team Safety in Convoys** — Maintain proper spacing, follow convoy instructions, and avoid dangerous maneuvers. Safe drivers keep everyone on the road protected and the convoy professional.

**Pro Tip:** Following these guidelines not only prevents accidents but also strengthens trust among drivers, keeps events running smoothly, and reflects positively on NextGear Logistics.
