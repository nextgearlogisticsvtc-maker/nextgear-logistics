# General Convoy Standards & Speed/Spacing

NextGear Logistics convoys are organized, safe, and professional. Following these standards keeps events smooth, enjoyable, and visible as a well-coordinated team.

### Convoy Rules
* Maintain 50–100m spacing — prevents collisions and allows smooth braking.
* Follow assigned positions/orders to keep the convoy structured.
* Obey Convoy Leader instructions at all times.
* Choose appropriate loads and keep trucks/trailers clean.
* Attend pre-convoy briefings (voice or written) for smooth coordination.

### Speed & Spacing
* Maximum speed: 90 km/h (adjusted for routes/conditions).
* Maintain safe distance (50–100m spacing) from the truck ahead.
* Avoid tailgating and sudden braking.
* Use cruise control when possible for steady speeds.

### Lane Discipline & Overtaking
* Stay in the right lane unless overtaking.
* Return to convoy lane immediately after passing.
* Overtake only if authorized by Convoy Leader or in emergencies.
* If falling behind, wait for regroup points — never speed dangerously.

### Rest, Fuel & Regrouping
* Follow scheduled rest/fuel stops announced before departure.
* Do not leave early; wait for the Convoy Leader's signal.
* If the convoy splits, regroup at the designated safe location.

### Communication
* Use Discord voice or agreed in-game CB channel 10 if not in Discord (only listening allowed).
* Report hazards, breakdowns, or traffic issues promptly.
* Keep instructions clear and concise, and always acknowledge commands.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Why it matters:** Following convoy standards ensures safety, coordination, professionalism, and a positive community reputation. Every driver contributes to making NextGear convoys reliable, smooth, and enjoyable for all.
{% endhint %}
