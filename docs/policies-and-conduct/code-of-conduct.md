# Code of Conduct

At NextGear Logistics, we pride ourselves on professionalism, respect, and teamwork in every mile we drive together. The following rules are in place to ensure a positive and safe experience for all members of our community.

### Professionalism on the Road
* Always drive respectfully and in accordance with TruckersMP rules.
* Avoid reckless driving, trolling, or intentionally causing accidents.
* Maintain safe distances, observe speed limits, and follow traffic laws.

### Respect for Others
* Treat all members of NextGear Logistics, as well as other drivers on the road, with courtesy.
* No harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or offensive behavior will be tolerated.
* Constructive criticism is welcome, but insults or personal attacks are not.

### Communication Standards
* Use English when communicating in company channels.
* Keep Discord chats and in-game communications respectful and free of spam.
* Follow instructions from company management and event coordinators.

### Attendance & Participation
* Remain active within the company by regularly participating in drives or events.
* Inform management if you need to take an extended break from activity.
* Join the official NextGear Logistics Discord server for coordination and updates.

### Membership Requirements
* Be at least 18 years old.
* Have at least 24 hours of playtime in Euro Truck Simulator 2.
* No bans in the last 6 months on TruckersMP.

### Equipment & Branding
* Use the official NextGear Logistics paint job, tag, and accessories when required for official events or convoys.
* Keep trucks well-maintained (both in-game and visually) to represent the company positively.
* Both modern trucks and classic trucks are welcome, as long as they meet event requirements.
* Only use any logo what makes our brand visual by formal acception.

### Feedback & Growth
* We value driver input — constructive feedback is encouraged to improve the company.
* Opportunities to grow within NextGear Logistics are available for dedicated and responsible members.

### Consequences for Breaking the Code
* Breaking these rules may result in warnings, temporary suspension, or removal from the company.
* Repeated violations will not be tolerated.
