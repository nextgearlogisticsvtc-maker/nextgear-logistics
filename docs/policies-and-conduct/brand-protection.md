# Protection of Company Brand Assets

To safeguard the integrity, security, and professional identity of NextGear Logistics, all employees and contractors are required to strictly protect the company's visual and operational assets. These assets include, but are not limited to:

* Official company logos
* Color palettes, branding elements, and design files
* Internal documents, procedures, and operational information
* Any non-public materials or resources belonging to NextGear Logistics

The sharing, publishing, forwarding, or distributing of any company asset or confidential information — whether internal or external, digital or physical, private or public — is strictly forbidden without explicit written authorization from CEO & COO.

Violating this policy constitutes a severe breach of trust and security. As such, any individual found responsible will be permanently blacklisted from NextGear Logistics without review, exception, or appeal.

{% hint style="danger" %}
NextGear Logistics maintains zero tolerance regarding the misuse of its branding, data, and identity. All employees must uphold this standard at all times.
{% endhint %}
