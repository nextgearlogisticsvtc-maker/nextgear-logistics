# Respect for Other Road Users

At NextGear Logistics, we value professionalism not just within our team, but also in how we interact with other players and VTCs on TruckersMP. Respecting others keeps convoys safe, enjoyable, and fair for the whole community.

### General Etiquette
* Convoys must never block roads, fuel stations, or junctions.
* Give space to non-convoy players and let them pass safely.
* Avoid spam honking or aggressive behavior — stay professional at all times.

### During Events, Partner Convoys & Meetings
* No VTC advertisements on the road.
* This ensures that all participating companies maintain equal visibility and respect.
* Helps prevent conflicts and keeps the focus on the event itself rather than promotion.
* Follow instructions from event or convoy leaders to maintain coordination.

### In-Game & Discord Respect
* Maintain a friendly, professional tone in all communications.
* Avoid disputes with other drivers or VTCs; report issues to staff if needed.
* Remember: your behavior reflects on NextGear and our partner companies.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Why it matters:** Respecting other road users and partner VTCs ensures safety, professionalism, and a strong community reputation. Every driver contributes to NextGear being known as reliable, courteous, and fair in every convoy or event.
{% endhint %}
